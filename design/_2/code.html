The Evolution of AI in Modern Design Systems

Published Oct 24, 2023 • 5 min read

In recent years, the integration of artificial intelligence into design tools has shifted from a novelty to a necessity. Designers are no longer just pushing pixels; they are curating generated outcomes and steering algorithmic processes.

This paradigm shift requires a fundamental misunderstanding of traditional workflows. El concepto de diseño generativo permite a los creadores explorar miles de iteraciones en segundos, something that was previously impossible without vast teams and unlimited budgets. The implications for rapid prototyping are profound.
